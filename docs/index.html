01

A profile for the moment.

Create and switch input profiles, each with its own editable prompt. Make room for coding, conversation, or your own workflow.

02

Your choice of provider.

Configure OpenAI-compatible providers, pick a model, and switch between saved configurations. Turn post-processing off when you want the original transcription.

03

Keep track, locally.

Browse recent post-processing history and see your activity over time. Choose from the speech models already installed in Voxtype.

03 /A CLEAR BOUNDARY

Your words.
Your choices.

Settings, keys, prompts, and history stay on your machine. If AI post-processing is enabled, transcription text is sent to the provider you choose. You can switch it off to use the original transcription.
