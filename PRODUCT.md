# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Omarchy Shell desktop plugin; a static web landing page introduces it.

## Users

Omarchy users who use Voxtype for speech-to-text and want to adjust their setup from the desktop bar.

## Product Purpose

Manage Voxtype input profiles, AI post-processing providers, installed speech models, and local history from a Shell panel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

AI post-processing is optional. The panel supports OpenAI-compatible providers and separate prompts for input profiles. History and statistics are local, and bypassed raw transcriptions are excluded. Post-processing sends transcription text to the chosen provider. Existing helper paths are currently specific to the original installation.
